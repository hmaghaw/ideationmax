What is PolyDial?

A SaaS telephony platform deployed on AWS that delivers AI-powered phone agents capable of natural multilingual conversations in over 60 languages. Businesses can automate inbound and outbound calls, handle customer inquiries 24/7, and integrate with Stripe for subscription billing.

The AI-augmented build story

PolyDial was built end-to-end by a single architect using AI tools as virtual team members. Replit and Windsurf served as backend accelerators. ChatGPT copilots handled UI prototyping. Manus AI automated market research, branding, pricing strategy, and tutorial video creation — proving that one senior engineer, amplified by AI, can replace an entire development team.

Technology stack

Containerized microservices architecture on AWS, Stripe-integrated subscription billing, and Twilio-powered telephony forming a production-grade global platform.
